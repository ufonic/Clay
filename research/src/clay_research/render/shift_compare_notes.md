# Blind comparison: four ways to shift pitch

Each folder is one vocal. `0_original` is the reference. **P, Q, R, S** are four different
pitch-shifting methods, each shifting the whole vocal up and down 2 semitones. The letters
mean the same method in every folder. Breaths are the original audio in all of them, and all
are level-matched, so only the sung notes differ.

Don't open `KEY.md` until you've picked.

**Listen for, on the held notes:**
- Phasiness or hollowness (the thing you heard in WORLD)
- Roughness, "doubling", or a gravelly buzz
- Smeared or softened attacks and consonants
- Does it still sound like the same singer?

**Reply:** rank P/Q/R/S best to worst (overall, or per vocal if it varies), and say if any is
free of objectionable artifacts at ±2 semitones.
