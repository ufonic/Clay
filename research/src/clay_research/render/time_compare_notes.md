# Blind comparison: three ways to change timing

Each folder is one vocal. `0_original` is the reference. **X, Y, Z** are three methods, each
playing the whole vocal 10% slower and 10% faster **without changing pitch**. The letters
mean the same method in every folder. All are level-matched.

Don't open `KEY.md` until you've picked.

**Listen for:**
- Smeared or softened attacks and consonants ("t", "k", "s")
- A reverb-like or "underwater" tail, especially when slower
- Phasiness or hollowness on held notes
- Vibrato that sounds steppy or unnatural when slower
- Breaths that sound wrong

**Reply:** rank X/Y/Z best to worst, and whether the best is free of objectionable artifacts
at ±10%.
